Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 1, 2025  |  Last updated: July 12, 2026

Barbro ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Barbro mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly:

  • Account information: name, email address (via Email OTP or Google Sign-In)
  • Profile information: phone number (optional, for verification), profile photo
  • Business information (barbers/owners): shop name, address, services, pricing, working hours
  • Booking data: appointment requests, queue positions, service history

We also collect information automatically or on demand:

  • Device identifiers for push notifications (FCM token)
  • Approximate location (when you grant permission, for finding nearby shops)
  • App usage and crash data (via Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Contacts (optional): phone number only, read from your device contacts to auto-fill a field — never uploaded to our servers

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the queue management and booking service
  • To send appointment confirmations and queue status notifications
  • To allow barbers to manage their team, services, and schedule
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs
  • To respond to your support requests

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Supabase: our backend provider for authentication, database, and storage
  • Firebase (Google): for push notifications and crash reporting
  • Google Sign-In: if you choose to sign in with Google

Your name and booking details are shared with the barbershop you book with, so they can serve you.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. You may request account deletion at any time from the app's Settings screen. Upon deletion, your personal data is removed within 30 days.

5. Children's Privacy

Barbro is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), Row-Level Security on our database, and OAuth 2.0 for authentication. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

You may request to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time by contacting us or using the in-app account deletion feature.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via the app or email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact Us
